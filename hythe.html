» HYTHE LAW (Stickied - Please Read!)

  1. Respect the Crown. Both Kings have equal authority. Do not try to play them against each other. (We know.)
  2. No griefing. Do not break, burn, blow up or "borrow" another villager's build.
  3. No lava, TNT or fire within the walls unless approved by the Sheriff.
  4. The island is Hythe's. Build outside the walls only with a permit.
  5. Keep the docks clear. Boats belong in slips, not in the middle of the harbour.
  6. Chests are private. Steal from one and face a royal trial.
  7. Be kind to visitors, especially our friends from Jippon.
  8. No spamming the forum, the shoutbox or the server chat.

Punishments range from a warning, to a fine, to banishment beyond the bridge. Kings' decisions are final. Mostly.
